# Server rules

Draft for operator review. It is not published to members yet. The rules of the
server are the operator's to set; this page proposes a short list and names what
is still open.

The list is short on purpose: a member reads it when joining. Longer
explanations belong in a rules channel or in the documents this page links.

## The rules

1. **Be respectful.** No harassment, no hate speech, no threats.
2. **Protect other people's privacy.** Do not share personal information about
   anyone without their consent.
3. **No sexual, gory or shocking content.**
4. **No spam.** No advertising, no unsolicited invitations, no repeated posts.
5. **No impersonation.** Do not pretend to be another person, a moderator or a
   project.
6. **No scams.** No phishing, no malware, no solicitation of money.
7. **Respect licences.** No pirated software and no help with getting it.
8. **Nothing illegal.**
9. **Keep to the topic of a channel.**
10. **The assistant is automated.** Its replies say so and can be wrong: check
    the sources it cites. Do not put secrets or personal data into a question.
    What it does with a question is in the [privacy notice](privacy-notice.md).
11. **Report security problems privately**, as [SECURITY.md](../../SECURITY.md)
    describes, not in a public channel.
12. **Moderators may remove content and time members out.** People decide every
    unclear case, never the bot alone.
13. **Discord's own rules apply.** You must follow Discord's Terms of Service
    and Community Guidelines and meet Discord's minimum age for your country.

## How the rules are applied

[ADR-0005](../adr/0005-moderation-and-escalation.md) fixes the order: the
platform's own moderation rules filter spam and obvious abuse, moderators decide
every unclear case, and the operator is the last instance. The bot assigns no
role and punishes nobody on its own judgement.

Rule 10 rests on the [AI disclosure](ai-disclosure.md) and on
[ADR-0002](../adr/0002-data-and-compliance.md).

## Open points for the operator

1. The final wording and the number of rules.
2. Rule 3: whether any age-restricted channel exists. The draft assumes none.
3. Rule 9: the language policy of the server, if there is one.
4. Rule 12: how a member appeals a moderator's action.
5. Whether these rules are also the code of conduct that ADR-0005 requires
   before moderation starts, or whether that is a separate text.
6. Where the published rules live and from where they are linked.
